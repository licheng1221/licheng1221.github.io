Hi, I’m Cheng.

Working across science and data.

My background spans bioinformatics, environmental research and remote sensing. I’ve worked with genomic and hyperspectral data, taken part in field campaigns across Europe, the US and China, and helped organise projects that bring people from different disciplines together.

I hold a PhD in Geography and Earth System Science from the University of Zurich and an MSc in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics from ETH Zurich and UZH. I have interned at Novartis in Basel and Xi’an Janssen in Beijing.

I’m interested in how scientific knowledge can be put to practical use. I value clear thinking, attention to detail, and working with people I can learn from.

Based in Zurich, Switzerland
